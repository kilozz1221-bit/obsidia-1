Our Philosophy

"Every object we create begins as silence — and becomes an expression of what cannot be said."

Founded in a small Milanese atelier in 2009, Obsidian was born from a single conviction: that objects of extraordinary quality deserve extraordinary restraint. We do not chase trends. We do not amplify. We refine, remove, and reveal.

Each piece in our collection is crafted by a single artisan, taking between four and forty weeks to complete. We use only materials that improve with time — leathers that patina, metals that age with grace, crystals that deepen with light.
